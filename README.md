# 🔄 My GRC Workflow

```text
Define Scope
      ↓
Identify Requirements
      ↓
Identify Assets, Processes & Risks
      ↓
Assess Likelihood and Impact
      ↓
Determine Inherent Risk
      ↓
Map Applicable Controls
      ↓
Review Control Design & Implementation
      ↓
Collect and Evaluate Evidence
      ↓
Test Control Effectiveness
      ↓
Document Gaps, Findings & Exceptions
      ↓
Determine Residual Risk
      ↓
Develop Remediation Plans
      ↓
Assign Owners and Due Dates
      ↓
Track Remediation
      ↓
Validate Closure
      ↓
Report Risk & Compliance Status
      ↓
Continuous Monitoring
